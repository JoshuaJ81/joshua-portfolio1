About Me

And then some.

  • Joshua Lavallais

    I am a training, documentation, and project coordination professional with extensive experience supporting technology and operational services in a healthcare environment. Throughout my career, I have worked at the intersection of people, processes, and technology—helping users adopt new systems, documenting procedures, coordinating projects, and supporting services from implementation through ongoing operations.

  • Implementation & Service Support

    In my current role, I manage and support several technology-based service lines, including digital signage, background music, and patient education. My work has included coordinating a large-scale digital signage platform migration, onboarding users across dozens of departments, developing training resources, collaborating with technical teams and vendors, maintaining procedural documentation, and helping translate technical processes into information users can understand and apply.

  • Project Coordination & Communication

    My background also includes client support, project coordination, technical writing, content development, and web design. Across these different areas, a consistent theme has been my ability to organize information, communicate clearly, learn new systems, and help move work from concept to completion. This portfolio highlights selected examples of my work in training, documentation, project coordination, technology implementation, writing, and digital projects.
